Choose a video fuser by the interface your graphics cards and monitor share, then by the top mode you need. HDMI 2.0 models cover 1080p 240 Hz and 1440p 144 Hz, DisplayPort 1.4 models carry 4K 120 Hz uncompressed, and HDMI 2.1 FRL 48G goes furthest but needs every device in the chain to support it. If you also want to record or stream the combined picture, pick a model with a capture output.

New to the idea? [What is a video fuser](/guides/what-is-a-video-fuser) explains how the main source and the keyed overlay source combine on one monitor.

## Interface bandwidth compared

| Interface | Data for video | Pixel clock at 8-bit RGB | Uncompressed ceiling, examples | Cable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HDMI 2.0 | 14.4 Gbit/s | 600 MHz | 1080p 240 Hz, 1440p 144 Hz, 4K 60 Hz | Premium High Speed HDMI (18 Gbit/s) |
| DisplayPort 1.4 (HBR3) | 25.92 Gbit/s | about 1080 MHz | 1080p 360 Hz, 1440p 240 Hz, 4K 120 Hz | VESA-certified, HBR3-rated |
| HDMI 2.1 FRL 48G | about 42 Gbit/s | about 1775 MHz | 1080p 550 Hz, 1440p 360 Hz, 4K 160 Hz | Ultra High Speed HDMI |

### HDMI 2.0

The simplest choice for HDMI monitors, presentation screens and signage displays. 1440p at 165 Hz needs about 679 MHz, past the 600 MHz limit, which is why HDMI 2.0 models stop at 1440p 144 Hz.

### DisplayPort 1.4

The common choice for high-refresh desktop monitors. 4K 120 Hz needs 25.8 of the 25.92 Gbit/s on offer, so it fits at 8-bit RGB with almost no margin, and 10-bit colour at that mode does not fit without DSC.

### HDMI 2.1 FRL 48G

The most bandwidth, but HDMI 2.1 features are optional. A port sold as HDMI 2.1 may run FRL below 48 Gbit/s, so check the graphics cards and the monitor before you choose the DC505's HDMI 2.1 mode.

## Why some vendor maxima need DSC

Display Stream Compression (DSC) 1.2, added in DisplayPort 1.4, compresses video up to 3:1, which VESA describes as visually lossless. Modes past the 25.92 Gbit/s HBR3 payload rely on it. 4K 144 Hz needs about 31.3 Gbit/s at 8-bit RGB, and the DC1A686-D3ZC's 1080p 639 Hz needs about 47 Gbit/s uncompressed.

DSC works only when both graphics cards, the video fuser and the monitor support it. Without it, a DisplayPort 1.4 model tops out around 1080p 400 Hz, 1440p 250 Hz or 4K 120 Hz. The [pixel clock and video fuser bandwidth guide](/guides/pixel-clock-and-fuser-bandwidth) has the full worked examples.

## Cables

A video fuser needs three video cables: one from each source and one to the monitor.

| Interface | Cable to use | Included |
|---|---|---|
| HDMI 2.0 modes | Premium High Speed HDMI, 18 Gbit/s | DC1A255-H3: two HDMI cables |
| HDMI 2.1 FRL 48G | [Ultra High Speed HDMI Cable](https://www.hdmi.org/spec21sub/ultrahighspeedcable) | None |
| DisplayPort 1.4 | VESA-certified cable rated for HBR3 | GODLIKE 4K Pro: two DisplayPort cables |

> [!TIP]
> Uncertified cables are a common cause of flicker or a black screen at high refresh rates. Buy rated cables before you blame the video fuser.

## What the sources need

- **Matching modes.** Set both sources to the same resolution and refresh rate. The DC1A686-D3ZC is the exception: it is vendor-listed for different resolutions on each source.
- **Overlay source graphics.** The GODLIKE 4K Pro lists an AMD RX 470, NVIDIA RTX 2060 or AMD Radeon 780M, or newer.
- **D60 4K.** The vendor lists an HDMI 2.1 output on the overlay source for high-refresh modes.
- **DC505 HDMI 2.1 mode.** FRL at 48 Gbit/s on both graphics cards and the monitor.

> [!NOTE]
> DisplayPort models need DisplayPort outputs on both sources. If a source only has HDMI, an HDMI model avoids adapters, which limit the link to their own rating.

## FAQ

### Is HDMI or DisplayPort better for a video fuser?

Use the interface your graphics cards and monitor share. DisplayPort 1.4 carries more than HDMI 2.0 (4K 120 Hz against 4K 60 Hz), and HDMI 2.1 FRL 48G carries more than both when every device supports it.

### Do I need an HDMI 2.1 video fuser for 1440p 240 Hz?

No. 1440p 240 Hz needs about 24.6 Gbit/s at 8-bit RGB, which fits DisplayPort 1.4 uncompressed. HDMI 2.0 is not enough.

### Which video fuser handles 4K 120 Hz?

The D60 4K, GODLIKE 4K Pro, DC1A686-D3ZC and DC505 all list it. Over DisplayPort 1.4 it fits at 8-bit RGB without DSC.

### Which models can capture the fused picture?

The DC505 and the DC1A686-D3ZC list 1080p 60 Hz capture with audio.

### Are the listed modes tested by TraceCoreLab?

No. They are vendor-listed maximums, and your monitor's timing decides what your chain reaches.
